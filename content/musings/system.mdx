on a podcast, peter steinberger describes the best users of coding agents are the folks who understand how software systems are built. it doesn't matter if you only know one language or don't know the ins and outs of a technology - knowing the system is more powerful.

peter obviously has a great grasp on systems. he mentioned how he wanted to build a project, and decided using `go` was the best language, even though he knew nothing about it. but that turned quite fine; the scaffolding mattered the most for outcome.

the same can be said for the future of society. those who knows how different parts come and work will attain the most wealth and power. labour and thought is commoditised, but knowing how to piece them together is what matters.

to end with napoleon
> "The amateurs discuss tactics: the professionals discuss logistics".
