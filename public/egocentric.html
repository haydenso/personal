Rows are the all-in rate per accepted hour. Columns are accepted hours per camera-day. Highlighted cells match your current sliders.

  • Your rate is paid in USD. Collector pay, owner fees, field staff and fixed costs are in KES, so a weaker shilling widens margin and a stronger one narrows it. Change the exchange rate at the top to test that.
  • Collector pay is a fixed KES amount per operator-day, so it costs more per accepted hour when yield is low. Informal daily wage is about KES 500, so the extra is shown as a percentage uplift.
  • Scout cost is a flat fee per qualified site spread over the accepted hours that site yields. Small businesses cap at 50 accepted hours, which is why many sites are needed.
  • Cash float is spend on collectors, owners and field staff over the payment lag (deployment length plus the 7 to 10 business days Human Archive takes to pay).
  • The Optimistic scenario assumes high yield, minimal owner fees and a lean team. Treat it as the upside to earn through the pilot, not as the plan to quote against.
